Approach

A clear process, from first call to final repayment.

We take the time to understand the opportunity, build the facility around how it will be repaid, and stay with you for its life.

Step 1

Introduce

A confidential conversation with you or your advisor about what you are trying to achieve and when. There is no cost and no obligation.

Step 2

Assess

We review your financial position, cash flow and any existing structures. We tell you quickly whether we can help, and if not, why.

Step 3

Structure

We design the facility with your accountant, lawyer and insurance advisor, starting with how and when it will be repaid. Terms are set out in plain language before anything is signed.

Step 4

Fund

Once documentation is agreed, funding comes from our private capital partners. Every facility is subject to underwriting, approval and definitive documentation.

Step 5

Steward

We review each facility at least once a year, monitor its covenants, and raise anything that may need to change early, while there is time to act.

Principles

Every structure starts with the exit.

We work alongside your advisors, not around them.
