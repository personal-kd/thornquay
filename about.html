About

A protected harbour for serious capital.

Thornquay Financial Corp. is a Toronto private financing firm. We arrange and fund specialized lending for professionals, business owners and families, and take selected positions in private opportunities.

The name

Thorn and quay.

A thorn guards what is valuable. A quay is the harbour wall where vessels moor safely and cargo changes hands. Together they describe what we do: structure capital carefully, and give it a safe place to be arranged and moved.

Our mark is three harbour arches rising from low to high, the tallest in brass. It stands for capital built patiently, one level at a time, and a goal reached and protected.

What we value

Discreet

Privacy is the default. We never name clients, deals or figures without written consent.

Structured

Every facility is designed around how it will be repaid, and documented in plain language.

Aligned

We work alongside your advisors and keep them informed for the life of the structure.
